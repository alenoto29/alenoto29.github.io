Call intelligence

The best call conversion of the year so far

I found out why people really call, and we changed the phone menu around it.

What changed
After the changes, the calls we studied converted at their best rate of the year so far, outside peak season.
What I did
Designed the analysis, guided the AI through it and checked a sample by hand.
Who uses it
My manager approved the changes. The team reordered the menu and trained operators.

The problem

Some options in the phone menu brought in calls with little chance of becoming a sale. Nobody knew which ones, or what stopped the rest.

The idea

Read only the opening of each call. That's where people say why they called, before an operator's pitch can change the picture.

How it works

  1. Read the openingAI says why each person called, using only the first lines.
  2. Connect it to the outcomeEach reason is matched with whether the call became a sale.
  3. Look at what went wrongFor calls that didn't convert: missing documents, eligibility, needs we couldn't meet.
  4. Check and actI compared a sample with the real calls, then turned the patterns into new menu priorities.

The honest bit

It's a before and after. The new menu, the training and the time of year could all have helped. I later rebuilt the workflow as a reusable tool; that version is separate from the analysis behind this result.

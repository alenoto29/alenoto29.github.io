Model evaluations

Same quality bar, smaller AI bill

I tested AI models on work I actually do, then moved to cheaper ones where they were good enough.

What changed
The best score didn't come from the most expensive model, and a much cheaper one came close. I now spend less on AI.
What I did
Picked five tasks from my SEO work and built the test with AI assistance.
Who uses it
Me, to decide which model does which job.

The problem

Using the biggest model for everything is easy, but cost and waiting add up. A cheaper one can also sound right while missing a link or changing a price.

A fair test

Five tasks I really do, with made-up data and fixed checks on what matters. Each model gets a score, shown next to its cost and time.

How it works

  1. Same tasks for everyoneEach model runs the same five tasks.
  2. Fixed checksEvery answer is checked for errors in facts, sums and structure.
  3. One scoreFive equally weighted task scores make a result out of 100.
  4. Quality next to costThe score sits beside the estimated cost and the time it took.

The honest bit

Each model ran once, so small gaps could move on a repeat. The checks can't fully judge how well a page reads, and I can't put a percentage on the saving.

Paid acquisition reporting

Budget decisions on one shared page

Ad spend, installs and activity lived in separate files. Now everyone reads the same numbers.

What changed
It became the app team's first shared view of these numbers, and it informs where the ad budget goes.
What I did
Built the dashboard with AI assistance, and I keep it updated.
Who uses it
The app team, from leadership to interns. Leadership asked for more metrics.

The problem

A new app needed one view of how its advertising was doing. The numbers lived in separate files, and the tracking tool couldn't bring the costs in.

When sources disagree

The ad platforms and the tracking tool reported different installs for the same days. I chose the platforms' own files as the reference, and kept the tracking figures in a separate, labelled view.

Missing is not zero

A file that hasn't arrived, a quiet day and a real zero stay three different things.

How it works

  1. CollectDaily files from the ad platforms and the tracking tool.
  2. CleanCode turns them into one consistent daily record.
  3. CheckRelated files must agree on totals. Guessed matches and timezone gaps are flagged.
  4. ShowAn interactive dashboard, with restricted access.

The honest bit

It's a reporting tool used in real decisions. It doesn't prove that moving the budget improved results, and it doesn't measure long-term profitability.

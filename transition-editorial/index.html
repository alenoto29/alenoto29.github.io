01 / About

How I approach
my work.

This portfolio shares how I see problems, how I work and what drives my choices. I study independently, keeping curiosity and creativity at the heart of what I build.

I start by breaking a problem down to its root, to understand where a change would make a difference. Then most of my time goes into shaping the solution: how the parts fit, how people use it and how it can last. Each project is a small ecosystem, shaped through thought, testing and reworking.

The fluidity in these paintings reflects that approach: working through complexity until the parts connect in a way that makes sense.

In SEO and growth teams, I’ve built workflows to improve results and save time on repetitive tasks, so more time goes towards priorities.

Below are some projects from the past nine months. I hope something catches your interest. I’m always open to a good conversation, a different perspective and a chance to keep learning.

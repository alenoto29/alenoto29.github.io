THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

A marketing mind.
A habit of building.

I’m Alessandro. My background is in SEO and marketing, and I now work in growth. I taught myself to use AI and build automations by working on problems around me.

I’m interested in the space between a useful idea and a tool people can actually use: understanding the process, choosing what to automate, and making the output easier to check.

AI helps me build and explore. My contribution is choosing the problem, shaping the approach and reviewing what comes out. Each project explains where colleagues made decisions, where AI contributed, and what the result can honestly support.
